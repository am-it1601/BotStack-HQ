## Brand & Style

The design system embodies **Precision Minimalism**—a design philosophy tailored for high-stakes enterprise SaaS environments where clarity, speed of thought, and architectural stability are paramount. The brand personality is professional, methodical, and technologically advanced, reflecting the rigorous requirements of compliance and operational workflows.

The visual direction prioritizes:

- **Architectural Rigor:** UI elements are structured on a strict grid, conveying a sense of "locked" stability and enterprise-grade reliability.
- **Cognitive Clarity:** Generous whitespace and a clear data hierarchy ensure that complex, multi-stage workflows (like compliance filing or lead scoring) remain digestible.
- **Technological Edge:** Subtle nods to AI capabilities are expressed through sharp accents of electric cyan, suggesting energy and automated intelligence without compromising professional trust.

This system moves away from playful consumer trends, opting instead for a utilitarian aesthetic that feels like a high-performance tool for serious business operations.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in **Deep Navy** and **Cobalt Blue** to establish an immediate sense of institutional trust and stability. These are contrasted against a **Vibrant Electric Cyan** which is reserved exclusively for AI-driven actions, status highlights, and primary interactive triggers.

### Color Usage

- **Primary (Deep Navy):** Used for typography, navigation bars, and structural foundations.
- **Secondary (Cobalt):** Used for primary buttons and active state indicators.
- **Tertiary (Electric Cyan):** An accent color representing the "AI Layer." Use it for progress bars, AI-suggestion icons, and high-energy touchpoints.
- **Neutral (Slate Gray):** Employed for secondary text, borders, and UI scaffolding to keep the interface clean and focused.

The system utilizes a dedicated **Semantic Status Palette** for compliance reporting:

- `Success (Green)`: For "Closed" phases and "Verified" documents.
- `Warning (Yellow)`: For "Pending" items or "Partial" data.
- `Error (Red)`: For "Out of Scope" or "Deadline Breached" states.

## Typography

The typography system relies on **Inter** for its neutral, highly legible, and systematic character. It ensures that data-heavy tables and complex labels remain accessible at any scale.

To reinforce the "Technical/Developer-Friendly" nature of the platform, **JetBrains Mono** is introduced for labels, status tags, and metadata. This monospaced font helps distinguish system-generated data (like ARN references or stock counts) from human-readable content.

### Hierarchy Rules

- **High Contrast:** Use Bold weights for headings to create a clear vertical rhythm.
- **Tabular Data:** Use `label-md` for table headers and `body-md` for cell content.
- **Semantic Italics:** Reserved for metadata, disclaimers, and legal parentheticals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a **Fixed Grid** philosophy for the desktop dashboard to maintain a "locked" and predictable interface, while adapting to a **Fluid Grid** for mobile views.

### Grid & Spacing Units

- **12-Column Grid:** Used for primary dashboard layouts.
- **Spacing Scale:** Built on a 4px base unit. Most components use 16px (gutter-sm) or 24px (gutter-md) padding to maintain "Precision Minimalist" breathing room.
- **Tabular Bias:** Content is organized into modular tables. When horizontal space is tight, lists must transition into structured tables to manage data density.

### Breakpoints

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fixed centered content with 48px page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Fluid margins (24px) and 8-column layout.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** Single column with 16px margins; typography scales to `-mobile` variants.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system uses **Low-Contrast Outlines** and **Tonal Layers** rather than heavy shadows to convey depth. This approach supports the "Precision Minimalist" aesthetic by keeping the UI flat and focused on data.

- **Surface Tiers:** Use subtle shifts in background color (e.g., White for cards vs. Light Gray for the background) to create separation.
- **Crisp Borders:** Elements are defined by 1px solid borders (`oklch(0.9288 0.0126 255.51)`).
- **Subtle Ambient Shadows:** Reserved only for floating elements like dropdown menus or modals. Shadows should be extra-diffused, using the Primary color (Deep Navy) at 5% opacity to maintain a clean, professional feel.
- **Interaction Depth:** On hover, components should increase border contrast or shift background tone rather than lifting via shadows.

## Shapes

The shape language is **Soft (0.25rem)**, striking a balance between industrial precision and modern approachability.

- **Containers:** Standard cards and input fields use a `4px` (0.25rem) radius.
- **Interactive Elements:** Buttons follow the same `4px` rule to maintain a consistent, modular look.
- **Status Pills:** Status indicators (Success/Warning) may use `rounded-xl` (12px) to differentiate them from functional UI blocks, making them appear more like "badges."

## Components

### Buttons

- **Primary:** Deep Navy background, white text, 4px radius. High-energy actions (AI triggers) use Electric Cyan.
- **Secondary:** Transparent background with 1px Cobalt Blue border.
- **Tertiary/Ghost:** No border, Slate Gray text; used for low-priority actions in tables.

### Input Fields

- **Precision Styling:** 1px border (`oklch(0.9288 0.0126 255.51)`), Inter body-md text. Active state uses a 1px Cobalt Blue border with a 2px soft blue focus ring.
- **Labels:** Always use `label-md` (JetBrains Mono) above the field for a technical, structured feel.

### Cards & Tables

- **Data Tables:** The core of the platform. Use alternate-row striping (Zebra) in very light gray for readability in high-density compliance views.
- **Status Badges:** Text-based badges using JetBrains Mono, with background colors reflecting the Semantic Status system (Green, Yellow, Red, Blue).

### Lead & Compliance Specifics

- **Timeline/Calendar:** Use vertical lines with circular status nodes to visualize the "Filing Calendar."
- **WhatsApp Preview:** A specialized card component that mimics the WhatsApp chat interface to preview agent interactions before they go live.
- **Action Hero:** For the landing page, use high-resolution, professional photography of modern office environments or architects at work, overlaid with crisp UI mockups.
